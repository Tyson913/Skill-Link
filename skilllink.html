How it works

Whether you need a job done or have a skill to offer, here is how SkillLink works from start to finish.

For customers

1. Search and filter

Browse by category or search by service, provider or category. Narrow the results by maximum price, minimum rating and pricing type.

2. Compare services

Open a service to see its price, rating, skills, availability and customer reviews. If it is not listed, use Request Service in the footer.

3. Ask or hire

Ask the provider a question through chat, then hire them. You need to log in or sign up to hire, chat or send a request.

4. Finish and review

Hired jobs appear in your History. Once a job is completed, you can rate it, write a review or report a problem.

For providers

1. Become a provider

Log in and choose Become a provider. We use the information in your profile and ask for anything missing in a short form.

2. List your services

Add each service with its category, price, pricing type, skills and description. You can edit or delete them anytime.

3. Manage your work

Your dashboard shows your total accumulated salary, overall rating and in-demand skills. Open a service to read its reviews and reply to them.
