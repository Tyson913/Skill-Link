About SkillLink

Connecting people who need things done with people who know how to do them.

Our mission

SkillLink makes it simple to find trustworthy help — whether that's a plumber down the street or a developer on the other side of the country. We built a two-sided marketplace where anyone can offer a skill or hire one, manual or technical.

How it works

Providers list what they do, set their rates, and get discovered through search and category browsing. Clients compare listings, filter by location and price, and reach out directly — no middlemen, no bidding wars.

Where we're headed

We're actively adding features like verified reviews, in-app messaging, and secure payments. Feedback is always welcome.

Help Center

Quick answers to get you moving — browsing, offering, and managing your account.

Getting started

Create an account with the Sign up button, then browse categories on the main page or search directly for a service or username.

Offering a service

Once you're signed in, list what you do under the relevant category, set your price, and add a location so nearby clients can find you.

Finding a provider

Use the search bar or category tiles to browse listings. The filter panel lets you narrow results by category, location, price range, and sort order.

Managing your account

Update your details from your profile page. If something looks wrong or you can't access your account, reach out through our Contact page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using SkillLink.

Is SkillLink free to use?

Creating an account and browsing listings is free. Any platform fees for specific transactions are disclosed before you're charged.

Can I offer more than one service?

Yes — you can list as many services as you like across different categories.

How do I contact a provider?

Open their listing to see contact details, or reach out once in-app messaging is available.

What if a provider doesn't deliver as promised?

SkillLink connects clients and providers but isn't a party to agreements made between them. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us and we'll remove your account and associated data — see our Privacy Policy for more on how it's handled.
